## 🧠 Calibrated Biomechanical Models & Mathematical Specifications

### 1. Shoulder Complex (Glenohumeral & Scapulothoracic)
* **2:1 Scapulohumeral Rhythm** (`kinematics.js` $\rightarrow$ `getScapulohumeralBreakdown`):
  * **Setting Phase (0° – 30°)**: Glenohumeral (GH) dominates (~26° GH, ~4° Scapula). Minimal scapular motion.
  * **Phase 2 & 3 (30° – 180°)**: Exact 2:1 ratio. For every 3° of arm elevation, 2° occurs at GH joint and 1° occurs at Scapulothoracic (ST) joint.
  * **Total Elevation (180°)**: 120° GH Abduction + 60° ST Upward Rotation.
* **3D Scapular Trajectory**:
  * Upward rotation (~55°–60°) at AC joint.
  * Posterior tilt (~15°–20°) to open the subacromial space.
  * Ribcage conformation translation (`joints['r_scapula'].position` hugs the cylindrical thorax so the inferior angle never flares or wings out).
* **Obligate Humeral External Rotation**:
  * Above 60°–90° abduction, the humerus rotates externally (~30°) to clear the greater tubercle under the acromion.
* **Subacromial Impingement Pathology**:
  * When scapula is locked (`isScapulaLocked = true`) or external rotation is restricted, greater tubercle impacts acromion at 60°–80°, turning the subacromial bursa pulsating red and triggering clinical warning alerts.

### 2. Foot & Ankle Biomechanics (Subtalar vs. Midfoot Complex)
* **Isolated Subtalar Motion** (Talocalcaneal joint alone):
  * `subtalar_inversion`: **0° – 20°** (AAOS: 20°–22°). Pure calcaneal medial varus tilt in the frontal plane. Midfoot remains neutral.
  * `subtalar_eversion`: **0° – 10°** (AAOS: 10°–12°). Pure calcaneal lateral valgus tilt in the frontal plane. Midfoot remains neutral.
* **Combined Foot Motion** (Subtalar + Midfoot / Transverse Tarsal TNCC & CC):
  * `subtalar_supination` (**0° – 35°**):
    * Subtalar inversion (20°) + Midfoot TNCC inversion (15°) + forefoot adduction + plantarflexion.
    * Elevates Medial Longitudinal Arch (MLA).
    * TNCC axes converge & lock at 36° $\rightarrow$ **Rigid Propulsion Lever**.
    * Closed chain (weight-bearing): Drives **Tibial External Rotation**.
  * `subtalar_pronation` (**0° – 20°**):
    * Subtalar eversion (10°) + Midfoot TNCC eversion (10°) + forefoot abduction + dorsiflexion.
    * Flattens MLA toward ground.
    * TNCC axes align parallel at 0° $\rightarrow$ **Flexible Shock Absorber**.
    * Closed chain (weight-bearing): Drives **Tibial Internal Rotation**.
* **Windlass Mechanism** (`first_mtp_extension`):
  * Extension of 1st MTP (0°–70°) winds plantar aponeurosis around metatarsal head, pulling calcaneus forward, raising MLA, and inverting hindfoot for push-off.

### 3. Hand & Wrist Biomechanics (Tenodesis & Prehension Grips)
* **Tenodesis Grasp Mechanism**:
  * Wrist extension (20°–30°) passively tensions FDP/FDS tendons, pulling fingers into functional grasp.
  * Wrist flexion releases fingers flat via extensor digitorum tension.
* **Thumb 1st CMC Saddle Joint**:
  * **Palmar Abduction** (0°–50°): 1st Metacarpal swings perpendicularly anteriorly (+X/+Z in hand coordinates) out of the palm plane.
  * **Flexion** (0°–15°): Sweeps medially across the palm with coordinated MCP & IP flexion.
  * **Opposition**: Sequential multi-planar abduction, medial flexion, and axial internal rotation meeting digit 5.
* **Prehension Grips** (`applyGripPattern` in `kinematics.js`):
  * `power_grip`: Cylindrical grasp, wrist ext 20°–30°, fingers tightly wrapped around object.
  * `tip_pinch`: Precision "OK" circle between thumb and index tips; digits 3–5 remain in a **relaxed, open resting cascade** (NOT curled into a fist).
  * `key_pinch`: Lateral pinch, thumb pulp presses against radial aspect of index middle phalanx.
  * `spherical_grip`: Cupped palmar arch with abducted digits holding a ball.
  * `open_hand`: Flat digital release.
* **Auto-Release on Motion Change**: Switching joint motions in the dropdown automatically resets `currentGrip` to `'none'` to prevent thumb/finger freezing.

### 4. Arthrokinematics Action Cards (Neumann & Kaltenborn)
* Located at the **very bottom of the Detail Panel** (`js/ui/detailPanel.js`).
* Formatted into **3 high-clarity Action Cards**:
  1. 🎯 **Motion Vectors**: Roll 🔄 (direction + arrow) & Slide ↔️ (direction + arrow) / Spin 🌀.
  2. ⚠️ **Clinical Significance**: 1-line practical risk summary (e.g. Subacromial impingement, ligamentous tear).
  3. 🩺 **Joint Mobilization**: Exact manual therapy direction used by clinicians to treat joint stiffness (e.g. Inferior Glide of humerus, Posterior Glide of talus).
  4. 🔒/🔓 **Close-Packed vs. Loose-Packed positions**.
* Dynamically updates according to every single joint action selected.
